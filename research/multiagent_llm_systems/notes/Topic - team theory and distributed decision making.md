# Topic - team theory and distributed decision making

Agents that share an objective but not information: common-payoff games, Dec-POMDPs, information structures, consensus and coordination, decentralised control, credit assignment.

- [[2024-02 Secret Collusion among AI Agents]] — Formalises secret collusion, where LLM agents hide their coordination from an overseer by steganography, and measures how well current models can do it and how well proposed defences hold. (also: [[Topic - strategic play among LLM agents]])
- [[2025-02 Multi-Agent Risks from Advanced AI]] — A taxonomy of the risks that appear only when many advanced AI agents interact: three failure modes, seven risk factors, thirteen illustrative case studies and research directions for each. - [[2026-02 Colosseum]] — Measures whether a subset of LLM agents in a cooperative team pursues a hidden side goal, by pairing a regret metric on actions with an LLM judge on messages, and finds that giving benign agents a secret channel is enough to trigger it. (also: [[Topic - strategic play among LLM agents]], [[Topic - communication topology and channels]])
- [[2026-04 Agent Q-Mix]] — Each agent in an LLM team picks its own communication action every round with a QMIX-trained policy, and the chosen actions together form the round's communication graph, trading accuracy against token cost. (also: [[Topic - automated pipeline and topology search]], [[Topic - communication topology and channels]])
