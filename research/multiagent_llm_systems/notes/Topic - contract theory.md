- [[2026-02 Intelligent AI Delegation]] — A conceptual framework (no system, no experiments) that treats delegation between humans and AI agents as more than task splitting: it adds authority transfer, accountability, trust and verification, and maps these onto nine protocol components for a future "agentic web". - [[2026-07 Moral Hazard in Multi-Agent Language Models]] — A text game built on Holmström's moral hazard in teams shows that LLM agents differ in whether they pay to help a teammate, that optimisation can reach full team success while skipping the costly help, and a counterfactual prompt optimiser (CREDIT) that rewards the help itself. (also: [[Topic - strategic play among LLM agents]], [[Topic - automated pipeline and topology search]])
