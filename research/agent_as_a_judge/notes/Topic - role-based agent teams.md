# Topic - role-based agent teams

Two or more LLM agents given complementary roles (instructor and assistant, analyst, coder and tester, a software company) work through a task by turn-taking dialogue or a fixed workflow. The subject is how role assignment, standard procedures, what each role sees and the termination rule keep the team on task, including role flipping, loops, hollow completion and drift.
