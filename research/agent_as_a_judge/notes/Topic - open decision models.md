# Topic - open decision models

Building, training, distilling, adapting, reproducing or releasing an open-weight decision model, or a recipe for getting one from an existing LLM or encoder. The contribution includes a model or a recipe, not only measurements of a hosted one.

Group: Decision models. See [[Topics]]. Same group: [[Topic - decision models]].

## Papers (37)
- [[2025-03 Rewarding Doubt]] — Fine-tunes an LLM with PPO on a log-scoring-rule reward so that the 0-10 confidence it verbalizes next to each answer matches its actual probability of being right. (also: [[Topic - calibration]], [[Topic - judges as reward signals]])
- [[2026-01 Calibration-Aware RL for Decision-Making LLMs]] — Shows that RL with verifiable rewards leaves reasoning classifiers near-certain on every verdict because the final decision token only copies the reasoning's conclusion, and fixes it by training that one token with a cross-entropy loss toward one-hot when right and uniform when wrong. (also: [[Topic - calibration]], [[Topic - judges as reward signals]])
- [[2026-02 Luna-2]] — Turns a small decoder-only LM into many cheap guardrail judges by giving each metric its own LoRA adapter that answers in one token, whose logit gives a confidence score. (also: [[Topic - trained judges and reward models]])
- [[2026-04 BERT-as-a-Judge]] — Fine-tunes a 210M-parameter encoder to classify a (question, candidate answer, reference) triplet as correct or incorrect, replacing regex answer matching at a fraction of the cost of an LLM judge. (also: [[Topic - trained judges and reward models]])
- [[2026-09 AnyJev]] — Reads a Jev-style typed decision (a probability over a fixed option list) from one prefill of a frozen open instruction-tuned LLM, and corrects the readout's label and position biases without any training. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - presentation sensitivity]])
- [[2026-09 Bongard]] — An open 4B-4B encoder-decoder decision model that reads a state once, answers many typed questions about it with a trained probability head, and is trained on one GPU through supervised judgments, contrastive alignment and sandbox outcome distributions. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-09 Chinese-Jev]] — A 322M-parameter encoder-only Jev-style decision model trained on 10 million Chinese decisions, then fine-tuned separately for medicine, law and finance, that matches or beats the hosted Jev on general Chinese tasks at a fraction of the latency. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-09 Dyad]] — Adds a separate action encoder to a pretrained LLM so that, on a special token, the model scores a dynamically supplied list of typed actions directly (System 1) while still reasoning in text (System 2) in the same policy. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-09 From Text Decisions to Pixels]] — PixelJev puts a Jev-style decision contract on small open vision-language models (Qwen3.5 2B/4B): an image, an instruction and a candidate list supplied at runtime go in, a valid candidate ID with probabilities over those candidates comes out, and the paper measures how far few-shot LoRA adaptation of that interface carries across recognition and multiple-choice VQA. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-09 Koa-decision]] — Fine-tunes an open LLM or VLM to answer any classification-style decision with one reserved control token, read off in a single decode step, so it matches frontier models on accuracy at about 0.53 s per request whatever the task. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-09 LAVOIR]] — Extends the open "System One" decision encoder Laya so that one forward pass returns both the decision distribution and, for each missing piece of information, how much asking about it would raise the probability of the correct answer, which lets the model decide whether to ask a clarifying question, decide, or hand off. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - abstention and fallback]])
- [[2026-09 OpenJev-RLCD]] — An open recipe for "reinforcement learning for calibrated decisions": it scores the answer distribution a reasoning model reads out after each of its own rationales with a proper scoring rule, calibrating that readout first and then reinforcing the reasoning with the same score. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]], [[Topic - judges as reward signals]])
- [[2026-09 PACT]] — Replaces plain cross-entropy in fine-tuning a single-token typed-decision model (Nimble-style, one logit read per allowed answer code) with four loss terms drawn from the contrastive-pair structure already in its training data; it lowers position bias and rubric error and steadies training, without raising accuracy. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - presentation sensitivity]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-09 this-that-model-1.0]] — A 1.88B open typed decision model that reads a calibrated distribution over caller-declared options from one hidden state, generating no tokens, so a program gets a 30.9 ms branch instead of an 8758 ms API call. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-10 AgriJev]] — Open, LoRA-adapted Qwen3.5 models (0.8B to 9B) that pick one of several described candidates for agricultural text and knowledge questions. They score all candidates in one forward pass instead of generating an answer, making them an open, domain-specific version of the hosted Jev decision interface. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 Audio System One Model]] — ASOM is a System One model for speech: a 6.2M-parameter reader over frozen Whisper and Qwen encoders that answers typed choice, score and yes/no questions written at request time about a clip, in one pass, each with a calibrated distribution trained on listener votes. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-10 Chinese Decision]] — Fine-tunes small Qwen3.5 causal LMs (0.8B, preliminary 2B) with LoRA into an open Chinese decision model that returns a calibrated probability distribution over supplied candidates from one forward pass, with one checkpoint serving general, medical, legal and financial decisions. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 FastJEV]] — A training-free way to make a JEV decision model score its candidates with less repeated work: shared state and token histories across candidates, plus candidate-side layers skipped by their effect on relative scores. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 From Retrieval to Typed Decisions]] — Converts biomedical retrieval encoders into calibrated one-pass typed-decision (System One) models, and shows that Laya's open RLCD recipe is a biased, rescaled estimator that loses to plain cross-entropy. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-10 FunDM]] — Trains a 1-5M-parameter head on two-thirds of a frozen Qwen3-8B to copy the full model's own answer probabilities, giving a label-free decision model (and a distilled agent-run judge) that keeps about 99% of the full model's accuracy at about 1.56x the throughput. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - trained judges and reward models]])
- [[2026-10 General Decision Models]] — Maps where single-pass decision models like Jev can replace LLMs, alone and composed into agents and simulations, and builds an open one by distilling an LLM's own reasoning into its first answer token. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - judge and decision-model audits]])
- [[2026-10 Intern-Decision]] — A family of 0.8B/2B/4B multimodal Jev-style decision models that answer several typed questions (choice, yes/no, score) over text and images in one causal forward pass, returning labels plus candidate probabilities instead of generated text. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 JevAgentHarness]] — Hands an agent's routine multiple-choice steps (which tool, which argument, whether to reflect) to a cheap decision model such as JEV, accepting its answer only above a per-choice confidence threshold fitted on the agent's own runs, and trains a 4B decision model on the states the agent actually visits. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - confidence-gated escalation]])
- [[2026-10 Jeva]] — A decision-native architecture for fast choose/yes-no/score judgments that encodes request and options separately, making decisions order-invariant, null-aware, scalable to thousands of options and cacheable across requests. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - presentation sensitivity]], [[Topic - abstention and fallback]])
- [[2026-10 LLM-as-Jev]] — Shows that an unmodified causal LLM, read through a numbered-option prompt and a prefix-free scoring trick, already works as a calibrated Jev-style decision model, and gives a KL-anchored fine-tuning recipe for the cases where training helps. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 MetaEncoder]] — Turns a 30B multimodal decoder into a cacheable bi-encoder that scores natural-language candidates against a free-form request, for both small-option decisions and million-item retrieval, and finds its ceiling is missing reasoning. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 OmniMed-Jev]] — Fine-tunes MedGemma-1.5-4B to return a distribution over a runtime candidate set instead of answer text, and shows its reported confidence tracks correctness far better than a matched generative baseline at similar accuracy. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-10 Prism-1]] — Shows that the only one-pass decision read that ignores option order and cannot be steered by an added option is a softmax over scores that each see one option alone, and builds that read into a fine-tuned LLM. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - presentation sensitivity]])
- [[2026-10 Qwen-Audio-Jev]] — Adds a typed decision head (CHOICE, NOUL, SCORE) to the Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash-Next audio LLM so that selection, yes/no judgment and rating are read out of one hidden state instead of generated as text,, about 20x faster than the original. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 Releasing the Inner Jev]] — Shows that an off-the-shelf instruction-tuned open LLM, read out from its answer-letter logits in one forward pass and temperature-scaled, already matches the closed decision model Jev, and that a short LoRA run on top beats it. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-10 SanSi]] — Turns a language model pre-trained to loop (Ouro-1.4B) into a typed decision model that reads option probabilities after every pass of the same layers, so one small model buys accuracy and reasoning depth with extra compute instead of extra parameters or generated text. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - adaptive reasoning depth]])
- [[2026-10 SecJev]] — Fine-tunes Kev's open Jev-style single-pass candidate scorer (0.8B to 9B) on a 14-task security corpus, so that one small model returns typed, probabilistic security decisions (injection present, quorum reached, logon action) and beats a general 9B decision model by 20.51 points. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
- [[2026-10 Selective Reasoning for Typed Decision Models]] — A single trained candidate-answer head on a frozen Qwen3.5-4B returns a typed choice either at once or from a partial chain of thought, with three small controllers deciding whether to start reasoning, when to query an answer, and when to accept it and cancel generation. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - adaptive reasoning depth]])
- [[2026-10 StartLux-Decision]] — A family of open decision models (0.8B to 27B) that answer typed questions in one forward pass and stop to reason only when unsure. Reasoning is trained with RLCD 2, which rewards the gain in proper-score quality of the decision probability and penalizes overlong traces. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - adaptive reasoning depth]], [[Topic - calibration]])
- [[2026-10 Student-Guided Teacher Distillation]] — A compact ModernBERT router, distilled from a DeBERTa-v3 zero-shot NLI teacher, also proposes the top-k candidate labels the teacher scores, so each labeling round gets cheaper and the student sharper, for routing requests into a fixed 60-category task taxonomy.
- [[2026-10 Verdict Encode Once, Decide Many]] — An open ModernBERT encoder (Verdict-MQ) that reads one piece of state once and returns a distribution for every typed question about it, with option-order and question-grouping invariance built into the attention mask. (also: [[Topic - decision models]], [[Topic - presentation sensitivity]])
- [[2026-10 VideoJev]] — VideoJev swaps a video MLLM's text-generating head for a shared scoring head that rates candidate time intervals or answer options against the video, so one encoded video can answer many grounding and multiple-choice questions in a single forward pass, at the accuracy of reasoning models and over 100x faster. (also: [[Topic - decision models]])
