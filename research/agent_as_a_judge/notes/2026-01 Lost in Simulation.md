# Lost in Simulation: LLM-Simulated Users are Unreliable Proxies for Human Users in Agentic Evaluations

## One line
User study (US, India, Kenya, Nigeria) on tau-bench retail showing simulated users are unstable and miscalibrated proxies.

## Method


## Evaluated on
Success shifts up to 9 pp by user LLM; simulators underestimate on hard and overestimate on moderate tasks; worst proxies for AAVE and Indian English speakers.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- One benchmark domain (retail).

## Limitations found by others
- (added by alternative-paradigms researcher) Human study (US, India, Kenya, Nigeria) on tau-Bench retail shows LLM-simulated users misestimate agent success and are worst proxies for AAVE and Indian English speakers.
