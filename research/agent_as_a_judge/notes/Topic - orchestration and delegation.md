# Topic - orchestration and delegation

A controller LLM decomposes a request, routes sub-tasks to tools, models or sub-agents, and composes their outputs: planner-executor, orchestrator-worker, hierarchical delegation, hand-offs. The subject is the architecture: planning, selection, what the controller sees of the workers' results and how it accepts them.
