# Scaling Laws For Scalable Oversight

## One line
Models oversight as games between mismatched-capability players with Elo scores and derives scaling laws for oversight success, including nested scalable oversight.

## Method
Validated on modified Nim, then four games: Mafia, Debate, Backdoor Code, Wargames. Fits Elo of guards and Houdinis against general model Elo; computes optimal number of nested oversight steps.

## Evaluated on
Nested scalable oversight success at a general Elo gap of 400: Debate 51.7%, Mafia 13.5%, Backdoor Code 10.0%, Wargames 9.4%; success declines as the overseen system gets stronger (from abstract).

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Games are simplified proxies; Elo-based modelling assumes a single capability axis.
