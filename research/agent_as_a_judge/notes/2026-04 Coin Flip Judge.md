# The Coin Flip Judge? Reliability and Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation

## One line
Repeated identical judging: pairwise verdicts flip 13.6% on average (28% of questions >20%), GPT-4o-mini picks A 72% of the time, cross-judge kappa 0.51, and ~11 trials are needed for a stable majority.
