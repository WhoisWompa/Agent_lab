# JEV-as-a-Judge: Accept When Confident, Escalate When Unsure

## One line
Decision-only judge outputs label probabilities and escalates low-confidence cases to a reasoning judge; cascade is 0.9 pts above a GPT-6 judge at 41% of its cost on 1,610 held-out pairs.
