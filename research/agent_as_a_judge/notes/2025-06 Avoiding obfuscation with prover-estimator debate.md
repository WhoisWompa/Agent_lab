# Avoiding Obfuscation with Prover-Estimator Debate

## One line
New recursive debate protocol that, under stability assumptions, lets the honest debater win against obfuscating strategies with compute comparable to the dishonest one.

## Method
Theoretical. One debater (prover) decomposes the claim into subclaims; the other (estimator) assigns probabilities to subclaims; recursion picks where to probe. Designed against the obfuscated-arguments problem where a dishonest debater forces the honest one to solve an intractable problem.

## Evaluated on
No empirical evaluation reported in the abstract.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Relies on a stability assumption on the arguments; not all problems satisfy it.
