# BrowseComp: A Simple Yet Challenging Benchmark for Browsing Agents

## One line
1,266 hard-to-find, easy-to-verify browsing questions with short answers graded by an LLM grader against the reference.

## Method
Inverted-question construction (answer first); short answer checked by model grader.

## Evaluated on
OpenAI Deep Research 51.5%.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Does not reflect the distribution of real user queries; answer uniqueness is not guaranteed.
