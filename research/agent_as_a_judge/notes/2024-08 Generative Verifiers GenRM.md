# Generative Verifiers: Reward Modeling as Next-Token Prediction

## One line
Trains the verifier with next-token prediction (P('Yes')) and optional chain-of-thought, so verification can use majority voting over sampled rationales.

## Method
- Data: model-generated solutions with correctness labels from final answers; CoT verification rationales synthetic (generated by LLMs, sometimes reference-guided).
- GenRM-CoT samples several verification rationales and averages the Yes probabilities.
- Unified with solution generation in one model.

## Evaluated on
- Algorithmic tasks and GSM8K, transfer to MATH; reports large best-of-N gains over discriminative verifiers, DPO verifiers and LLM-as-a-judge, scaling with model size and number of votes.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Tested on math/algorithmic domains with checkable answers.
- Inference cost grows with number of rationales.
- Reasoning domains with verifiable answers.

## Limitations found by others
- ProcessBench (arXiv:2412.06559) and the Qwen PRM lessons paper show outcome-trained verifiers accept correct answers with flawed steps.

## What it leaves untried
- Open-ended, non-verifiable tasks.
