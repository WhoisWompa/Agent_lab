# PiCO: Peer Review in LLMs based on the Consistency Optimization

## One line
Unsupervised peer evaluation: models answer unlabeled questions and score each other; per-model capability weights are optimised so stronger models' votes count more.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Rests on the assumption that stronger models judge more accurately and also answer better; breaks if a model is a good answerer but biased reviewer.

## Limitations found by others
- Goel et al. 2025 (arXiv 2502.04313): similar models prefer each other, which undermines the independence of peer reviewers.
