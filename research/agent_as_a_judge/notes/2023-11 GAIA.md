# GAIA: a benchmark for General AI Assistants

## One line
466 assistant questions needing browsing, tools and multi-modality, each with a short unambiguous answer graded by quasi-exact match.

## Method
Verifier = normalized exact match of final answer; trajectory not judged.

## Evaluated on
Humans 92% vs GPT-4 with plugins 15% at release.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Final-answer-only scoring ignores how the answer was found.

## What it leaves untried
- Judging the search path, not just the final string.
