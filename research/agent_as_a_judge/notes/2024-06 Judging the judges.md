# Judging the Judges: Evaluating Alignment and Vulnerabilities in LLMs-as-Judges

## One line
On TriviaQA with human labels, only the largest judges approach human alignment; percent agreement hides differences that Cohen's kappa reveals; judges are lenient and sensitive to prompt length and dummy answers. Closest match found for the brief's 'Humans as judges of judges'.
