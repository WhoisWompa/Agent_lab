# LLMs Cannot Reliably Judge (Yet?): A Comprehensive Assessment on the Robustness of LLM-as-a-Judge

## One line
RobustJudge tests 15 attacks and 8 defences on 13 judges: judges are vulnerable under pointwise and pairwise protocols, no defence dominates on robustness, utility and cost, and a production judge (PAI-Judge) is inflatable.
