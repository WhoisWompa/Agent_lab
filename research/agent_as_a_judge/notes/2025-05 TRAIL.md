# TRAIL: Trace Reasoning and Agentic Issue Localization

## One line
Benchmark of 148 human-annotated agent traces (single and multi-agent, SWE and open-world search) with an error taxonomy; LLMs must locate and categorise errors.

## Method
Traces in OpenTelemetry form from GAIA and SWE-bench style agents; taxonomy of reasoning, system-execution and planning/coordination errors; judges read long traces.

## Evaluated on
Best model Gemini-2.5-pro scores only 11%; long-context LLMs do poorly at trace debugging.
