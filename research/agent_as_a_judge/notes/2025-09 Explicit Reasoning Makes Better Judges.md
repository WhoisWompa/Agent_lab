# Explicit Reasoning Makes Better Judges: A Systematic Study on Accuracy, Efficiency, and Robustness

## One line
Small Qwen3 thinking judges beat non-thinking ones by ~10 points on RewardBench at <2x cost and are ~6% more consistent under positional, bandwagon and other biases; few-shot gives less at >8x cost.
