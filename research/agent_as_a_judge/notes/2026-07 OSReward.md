# OSReward: Instituting Standardized Evaluation for Cross-Platform Computer-Use Reward Models

## One line
Benchmark for VLM judges / reward models on computer-use trajectories with multi-stage human verdicts; releases OS-Shepherd-100K and OS-Shepherd 9B/35B judges.

## Evaluated on
Pilot: best VLM judges disagree with benchmarks' own verifiers on ~a quarter of desktop verdicts. Judges show leniency bias (fail runs labelled success). OS-Shepherd claimed to match commercial judges at 30-60x lower cost.
