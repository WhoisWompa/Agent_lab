# τ²-Bench: Evaluating Conversational Agents in a Dual-Control Environment

## One line
Extends tau-bench with a telecom domain where the user also has tools and acts on a shared state, plus a compositional task generator.

## Method
User simulator constrained by its own tools and observable state; outcome graded on final state.

## Evaluated on
Reports significant drops from no-user to dual-control mode.
