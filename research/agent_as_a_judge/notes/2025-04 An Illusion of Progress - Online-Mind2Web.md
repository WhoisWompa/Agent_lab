# An Illusion of Progress? Assessing the Current State of Web Agents

## One line
Online-Mind2Web (300 live tasks, 136 websites) plus WebJudge, an LLM-as-a-judge that reaches ~85% agreement with humans; finds prior web-agent results over-optimistic.

## Method
WebJudge: LLM judge over key screenshots and actions, identifying key points of the task first. Human evaluation as reference.

## Evaluated on
~85% agreement with human judgement, higher than prior auto-eval methods. Agents score much lower than earlier reported numbers suggested.
