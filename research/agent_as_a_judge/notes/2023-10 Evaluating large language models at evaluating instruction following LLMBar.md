# Evaluating Large Language Models at Evaluating Instruction Following

## One line
LLMBar meta-evaluation set of adversarial pairs where the superficially better output fails the instruction; judges fall for surface quality.

## Method


## Evaluated on
- (llm-judge-foundations) Vanilla accuracy Natural/Adversarial: ChatGPT 79.0/32.0, LLaMA-2-70B-Chat 77.0/32.6, PaLM2 82.0/60.7, GPT-4 93.5/73.1. Human experts 94% agreement. GPT-4 Adversarial: Rules 80.8, Swap 80.1 (positional agreement 89.0 -> 96.0), Metrics+Reference 82.8. CoT hurt: 76.6 vs 80.8. Reward/preference models (AlpacaFarm RMs, SteamSHP) 26-38% on Adversarial, i.e. below chance.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Instruction-following pairs only.
- Adversarial set filtered with ChatGPT-based evaluators; GPTOut subset generated by GPT-4 (possible advantage).
- Only 419 instances, single-turn, instruction following only.
- Falcon-180B omitted for strong positional bias.

## Limitations found by others
- Soumik 2026 (2604.23178) uses LLMBar (n=200) among three benchmarks and finds style bias, not position bias, dominates in 2025-26 frontier judges.

## What it leaves untried
- Multi-turn and long-form instruction following; checking whether reasoning-model judges still fail the adversarial split.
