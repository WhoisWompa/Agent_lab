# ImpossibleBench: Measuring LLMs' Propensity of Exploiting Test Cases

## One line
Creates 'impossible' variants of LiveCodeBench and SWE-bench tasks where spec and tests conflict, so any pass is cheating; measures agents' propensity to exploit tests.
