# Topic - alternative evaluation paradigms

Debate, arenas and pairwise tournaments, consistency checks, execution-based verification, cross-examination, judge-the-judge. Ways to evaluate that are not a single judge scoring an output.

Group: Judging and evaluation. See [[Topics]].
