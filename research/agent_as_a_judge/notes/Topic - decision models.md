# Topic - decision models

Typed, System One, single-pass decision models (Jev and its kin: Laya, Kev, Clef, Decider, Bongard): architecture, theory, interface, properties, and their use inside agents or pipelines. Any paper whose subject is such a model.

Group: Decision models. See [[Topics]]. Same group: [[Topic - open decision models]].

(also: [[Topic - judge and decision-model audits]], [[Topic - confidence-gated escalation]])
