# Confirmation bias: A challenge for scalable oversight

## One line
Two human studies find simple oversight protocols (including seeing arguments for both answers) give no overall accuracy advantage, and humans grow more confident in the model even when it is wrong.

## Method
Human evaluators told the model is usually but not always correct; compare protocols such as showing arguments for both answers vs the model's answer alone; Study 2 lets participants search online.

## Evaluated on
No overall advantage of protocols. Arguments for both sides helped only when the model was wrong. After online research, confidence in the system rose even for incorrect answers. Reanalysis of more optimistic earlier work attributes gains to evaluators' outside knowledge, which shrinks as models improve.

## Limitations admitted by the authors
- Simple protocols only; small human studies.
