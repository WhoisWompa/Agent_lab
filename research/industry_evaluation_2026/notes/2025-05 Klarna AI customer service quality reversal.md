# Klarna reverses on AI-only customer service

## One line
May 2025 reports that Klarna's CEO said cost had been "too predominant" an evaluation factor for its AI assistant, producing "lower quality", and that it would rehire human support.

## What they do
Klarna had claimed (2024) its assistant did the work of 700 agents with CSAT equal to humans; in 2025 it began recruiting flexible human agents. Klarna disputed "reversal" framing: "Klarna is not reversing on AI." Other coverage: https://www.computing.co.uk/news/2025/ai/meat-back-on-the-menu-as-klarna-rows-back-on-ai.

## Evidence
CEO quotes via Bloomberg interview, repeated in multiple outlets; headcount figures vary between reports.

## Limitations and criticisms
Second-hand reporting; no internal eval data published.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Canonical failure story of optimising a cost/deflection metric that did not track user-perceived quality: a Goodharted top-line metric.
