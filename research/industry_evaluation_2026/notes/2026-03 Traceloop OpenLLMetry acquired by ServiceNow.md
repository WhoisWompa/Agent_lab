# Traceloop / OpenLLMetry (acquired by ServiceNow)

## One line
Traceloop, maker of the Apache-2.0 OpenLLMetry OTel instrumentation (7.5k stars), joined ServiceNow (completed 5 May 2026) to power AI Control Tower runtime observation of agents.

## What they do
OpenLLMetry instruments LLM/agent frameworks into OpenTelemetry GenAI spans; Traceloop platform added evals/monitoring. ServiceNow press release: https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-expands-AI-Control-Tower-to-discover-observe-govern-secure-and-measure-AI-deployed-across-any-system-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx

## Evidence
GitHub traceloop/openllmetry 7,501 stars (GitHub API 2026-10-20).

## Limitations and criticisms
Standalone product future unclear; OpenLLMetry remains Apache 2.0.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Listed only; part of the 2026 consolidation of tracing into enterprise platforms.
