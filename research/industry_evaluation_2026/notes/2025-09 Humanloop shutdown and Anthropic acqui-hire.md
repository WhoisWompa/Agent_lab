# Humanloop shutdown (team joined Anthropic)

## One line
Humanloop, an early prompt-management and eval platform, stopped billing 30 Jul 2025 and was sunset 8 Sep 2025 after its team joined Anthropic.

## What they do
Prompt management, evals with LLM/code/human evaluators, monitoring. Team joined Anthropic Aug 2025 (no product/IP taken per TechCrunch: https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/13/anthropic-nabs-humanloop-team-as-competition-for-enterprise-ai-talent-heats-up/).

## Evidence
Dates from https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/llm-observability-acquisitions-2026 citing Humanloop's homepage and migration guide.

## Limitations and criticisms
Customers had ~6 weeks to migrate — canonical example of eval-platform lock-in risk.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Listed only; illustrates that eval history in a hosted tool is fragile.
