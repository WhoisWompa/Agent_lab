# ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system certification

## One line
ISO/IEC 42001 is the certifiable management-system standard for AI. Anthropic was among the first frontier labs certified, in January 2025. The standard audits governance processes, not model capability.

## What they do
- Certification was issued by Schellman, which is accredited by ANAB.
- Anthropic says the audit covered:
  - policies for responsible design and deployment;
  - testing and monitoring;
  - transparency;
  - defined roles and oversight ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-achieves-iso-42001-certification-for-responsible-ai)).

## Evidence
- Anthropic frames certification as validating its existing practices, including its Responsible Scaling Policy.
- Renewal status as of October 2026 was not confirmed.

## Limitations and criticisms
- The standard requires a risk assessment and evidence of testing, but sets no evaluation content, no benchmarks and no thresholds.
- The standard's text was not consulted.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
ISO 42001 is relevant to enterprise procurement: it asks whether an organisation has a testing and monitoring process. It says nothing about whether a given agent works. Evaluation evidence from judges would feed into such a process as records.
