# How Notion evaluates AI at scale (Braintrust case study)

## One line
Vendor case study claiming Notion's eval workflow on Braintrust took it from fixing ~3 AI issues a day to ~30, across 70 engineers, with new frontier models deployable in under 24 hours.

## What they do
Notion (Sarah Sachs, AI modelling lead) evolved from prompt-and-judge setups to a broader eval framework on Braintrust; Stripe and Zapier also reported as Braintrust users.

## Evidence
3->30 issues/day reported by secondary summaries (https://www.voiceflow.com/blog/what-is-braintrust, plushcap); not confirmed in the primary excerpt. Marketing.

## Limitations and criticisms
Marketing; no methodology; no Notion-authored engineering post found.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Indicates that big product companies run evals on vendor platforms and value fast model swaps (<24h) as a main payoff.
