# TruLens

## One line
Open-source (MIT, 3.6k stars) LLM app evaluation and tracing library known for the RAG Triad feedback functions, now Snowflake-backed.

## What they do
Feedback functions (groundedness, context relevance, answer relevance), OTel tracing, agent GPA-style metrics.

## Evidence
GitHub 3,602 stars, last push 2026-10-19.

## Limitations and criticisms
Smaller community than DeepEval/Ragas.
