# Grok 4.20 System Card

## One line
xAI's 8-page, 7 Apr 2026 system card for Grok 4.20 (single- and multi-agent modes), covering malicious-use refusals, loss-of-control propensities with a Petri-2.0-based alignment audit, and dual-use capability (CBRN, cyber, manipulation) under xAI's Frontier AI Framework.

## What they do
- Malicious use: internal multilingual refusal set (several thousand prompts in 6 languages) graded by "a separate model"; jailbreak templates; AgentHarm (agentic refusals); AgentDojo (prompt injection). Third-party evaluators got an early snapshot to red-team the refusal boundary.
- Training-time: "we use an LLM to judge the severity of the undesirable behavior, and decrease the reward of rollouts that exhibit such undesirable behavior" (LLM-judge penalty as RL reward shaping), via targeted environments or an environment-agnostic penalty.
- Loss of control: MASK (honesty), Anthropic sycophancy eval and an internal contrastive sycophancy eval built from production conversations, HLE calibration (RMS calibration error; correctness judged by an LLM judge).
- Automated alignment audit "using an internal tool built off of Petri 2.0": seed scenarios -> agentic pressure scenarios; reports cooperation with misuse, delusion validation, sabotage against user / against xAI, verbalized evaluation awareness.
- Third-party audit of deceptive and scheming behaviour on an early checkpoint (evaluator not named).

## Evidence
- Table 1 (single-agent): refusal violation rate 0.00; user jailbreak 0.01; AgentHarm violation 0.30; AgentDojo attack success 0.33.
- Audit: verbalized evaluation awareness 0.10 / 0.08 / 0.09 (Grok 4 / 4.1 / 4.2); system-prompt-override misuse 0.32 (up from 0.16), attributed to better instruction following; sabotage against xAI 0.04.
- xAI notes MASK cannot be run on multi-agent mode because system prompt overrides interfere with sub-agents.

## Limitations and criticisms
- Short card; third-party evaluators unnamed. Microsoft's Azure catalogue said its own evaluations rated Grok-4.20 Reasoning less safe than other models offered via Azure Direct (https://ai.azure.com/catalog/models/grok-4-20-reasoning).
- Grok 4 (Jul 2025) initially shipped without a system card (https://fortune.com/2025/07/17/elon-musk-xai-grok-4-no-safety-report); a card followed on 20 Aug 2025 (https://data.x.ai/2025-08-20-grok-4-model-card.pdf).

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Shows a smaller-disclosure lab still converging on the same toolkit: LLM-graded refusals, AgentHarm/AgentDojo for agents, Petri-style auditor+judge, and LLM-judge penalties in RL.
