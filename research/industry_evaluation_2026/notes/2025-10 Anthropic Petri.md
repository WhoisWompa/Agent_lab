# Petri: an open-source auditing tool to accelerate AI safety research

## One line
Anthropic's open-source (reported MIT, built on UK AISI's Inspect) Parallel Exploration Tool for Risky Interactions: an auditor agent runs multi-turn scenarios with simulated users/tools against a target and a judge scores transcripts; pilot ran 14 models on 111 scenarios. By 2026 Petri 2.0/3.0 is used inside xAI's (Grok 4.20) and Meta's (Muse Spark 1.1) alignment audits.
