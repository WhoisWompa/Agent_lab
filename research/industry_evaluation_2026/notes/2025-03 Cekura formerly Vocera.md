# Cekura (formerly Vocera): voice/chat agent testing and monitoring

## One line
Cekura (YC F24; renamed from Vocera in Mar 2025; $2.4M seed mid-2025) simulates test conversations with personas for voice/chat agents and monitors production calls.

## What they do
Scenario generation, custom personas, alerts on latency spikes or missed function calls; integrations with LiveKit, Pipecat, Vapi, Retell, ElevenLabs, Telnyx; $30/mo self-serve and 'Conditional Actions' for deterministic tests (per Coval comparison).

## Evidence
Funding: https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/cekura-raises-2-4m-for-ai-agents . Claimed 2,000+ concurrent simulations (self-reported).
