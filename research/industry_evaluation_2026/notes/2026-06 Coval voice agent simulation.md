# Coval: simulation and evaluation for voice and chat agents

## One line
Coval (YC) raised a $28M Series A led by Norwest (June 2026; $31M total) for simulation-first testing, observability and labelling of voice and chat agents.

## What they do
Synthetic callers across thousands of scenarios, CI/CD regression, transcripts/workflows as scenario inputs; customers incl. Zoom, Deepgram (https://pulse2.com/coval-raises-28-million-series-a-to-advance-voice-ai-evaluation-platform/).

## Evidence
Claims: reduces manual QA up to 30x, deployment up to 10x faster — vendor marketing.

## Limitations and criticisms
Closed-source SaaS; comparisons in this niche are mostly vendor-written (e.g., Coval's own Hamming vs Cekura post https://www.coval.ai/blog/hamming-vs-cekura).

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Voice-agent simulation is the most mature 'agent simulation' sub-market.
