# Helicone (maintenance mode after Mintlify acquisition)

## One line
Helicone, an open-source LLM gateway/observability tool, joined Mintlify (announced 3 Mar 2026) and is in maintenance mode.

## What they do
Proxy-based logging, cost tracking, basic evals. Founders: service will 'remain live for the foreseeable future in maintenance mode'.

## Evidence
Per https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/llm-observability-acquisitions-2026 and https://www.mintlify.com/blog/mintlify-acquires-helicone

## Limitations and criticisms
Maintenance mode = no new eval features.
