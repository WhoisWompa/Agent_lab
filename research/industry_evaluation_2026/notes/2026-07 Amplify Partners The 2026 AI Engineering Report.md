# The 2026 AI Engineering Report

## One line
Survey of 1,000+ AI engineers (page dated 07-02-2026, ambiguous day/month) on the AI stack, incl. eval pain and agent failures.

## What they do
Survey; charts not readable in fetch.

## Evidence
Evals, orchestration/agent logic, inference and security are "within a few points of each other" as biggest stack challenge; only 4% say their stack works well; quality and task success are the most monitored production items; ~half monitor cost; 61% say hallucinations are where agents fail or frustrate most; top guardrails are human-in-the-loop approvals and gated permissions; "no consensus" on monitoring or guardrails. Notion landing: https://www.notion.com/lp/ai-engineering-survey.

## Limitations and criticisms
VC survey; exact eval-adoption percentages were in charts not captured.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Corroborates that evals remain an unsolved pain and that human approval gates, not evals, are the main agent safety net in practice.
