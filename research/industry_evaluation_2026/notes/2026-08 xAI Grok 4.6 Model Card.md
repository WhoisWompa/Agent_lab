# Grok 4.6 Model Card

## One line
xAI's 12 Aug 2026 (rev. 17 Aug) 42-page model card for Grok 4.6, built 'in collaboration with Cursor', whose capability section is almost entirely agentic coding, knowledge-work, engineering and R&D benchmarks, many run by third-party harnesses.

## What they do
- Coding: CursorBench 3.2, APEX-SWE, FrontierCode v1.1 (graded "against maintainer-authored rubrics, verifiers, and hard blocker criteria"), DeepSWE v1.1, SWE-Marathon v1.1 (results from Abundant AI), Terminal-Bench 3.0.
- Knowledge work: AA GDPval (GDPval-AA v2, Artificial Analysis harness, pairwise quality ratings), AA-Briefcase (multi-week projects; rubric pass rate + pairwise judgments), APEX-Agents, OfficeQA Pro, Legal Agent Benchmark (Vals AI 'Valkyrie' harness, expert all-pass rubrics).
- Engineering: EEBench, 3DCodeBench, PartBench, CADGenBench, CADBench. R&D: 'SpaceXAI MTS Eval', InferenceEval, KernelBenchInternal v1.1.
- Factuality: internal hallucination benchmark, "a separate grader flags factually unsupported statements" (Grok 4.6 1.7%).
- Safety: cyber (CyberGym, CVE-Bench, SecureCodeReview, HackerBench v0.2), bio/chem (VCT, BioUseBench, WMDP, LAB-Bench, ProtocolQA, BixBench), jailbreaks, general refusals (grader model), child safety, self-harm, MASK-Rectified, sycophancy. Unrestricted config given to third-party evaluators who "corroborated" internal cyber results.

## Evidence
- General refusal compliance 0.93% (vs 1.10% Grok 4.5). GDPval-AA 1753 Elo; FrontierCode 61.3%.
- Changelog corrected HackerBench, self-harm, MASK and LAB results after publication.

## Limitations and criticisms
- No automated alignment audit or evaluation-awareness section appears in the table of contents (unlike Grok 4.20); I read the TOC and excerpts only.
- Many evals are internal or vendor-run; external evaluators unnamed.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Illustrates the 2026 shift to third-party rubric/pairwise graders for professional deliverables (Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, Abundant AI) and maintainer-rubric grading for code quality beyond pass/fail tests.
