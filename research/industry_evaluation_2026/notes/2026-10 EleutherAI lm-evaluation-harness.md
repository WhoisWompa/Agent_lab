# lm-evaluation-harness

## One line
The standard open-source harness for few-shot model benchmarks (14.3k stars, MIT), used by the Open LLM Leaderboard; model-level, not app or agent evaluation.

## What they do
Hundreds of tasks, loglikelihood and generation scoring, many backends.

## Evidence
GitHub 14,273 stars, last push 2026-09-14 (GitHub API).

## Limitations and criticisms
Not designed for agents or LLM-as-judge app evals.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Listed only; model benchmarking layer.
