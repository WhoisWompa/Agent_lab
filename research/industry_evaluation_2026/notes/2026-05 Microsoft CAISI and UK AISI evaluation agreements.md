# Advancing AI evaluation with CAISI (US) and the AI Security Institute (UK)

## One line
On 5 May 2026 CAISI signed pre-deployment testing agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft and xAI (joining OpenAI and Anthropic, five labs total), covering cyber, bio and chemical risks plus post-deployment assessment; Microsoft announced a parallel UK AISI agreement. Secondary sources say CAISI has done 40+ evaluations incl. unreleased models.
