# Decagon Simulations

## One line
Decagon's Sep 2025 launch of simulated-user testing built around Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs).

## What they do
AI-generated conversations from mock users across chat, email, voice; user defines success criteria; reports whether the agent reached it; historical failed transcripts converted into test cases; voice conditions simulated. Product page: https://decagon.ai/product/testing-qa.
