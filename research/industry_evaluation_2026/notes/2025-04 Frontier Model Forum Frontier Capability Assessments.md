# Frontier Model Forum: Frontier Capability Assessments

## One line
An industry body made up of the frontier labs. Its technical reports, published 22 Apr 2025 with a follow-up in August 2025, set out shared practice for assessing dangerous capabilities and for using third-party assessors.

## What they do
- The April 2025 report describes how assessments are built:
  - threat modelling with domain experts;
  - a range of evaluations, which differ widely in cost and in the strength of evidence they give;
  - relative assessments that compare a new model with ones already evaluated ([FMF](https://frontiermodelforum.org/technical-reports/frontier-capability-assessments/)).
- The focus is on CBRN and advanced cyber risks.
- The August 2025 technical report covers third-party assessments ([PDF](https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/uploads/2025/08/FMF-Technical-Report-on-Third-Party-Assessments-2.pdf)).
- 2026 outputs:
  - "Managing Advanced Cyber Risks in Frontier AI Frameworks" (13 Feb 2026).
  - "Frontier AI and Nuclear Security" (30 Jan 2026) ([publications](https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/publications)).

## Evidence
- The Forum says the science is moving fast and the report will go out of date.
- No 2026 revision of the capability-assessment report was found.

## Limitations and criticisms
The reports are written by the labs about themselves and are descriptive, not binding.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
These reports set out the shared industry vocabulary for capability thresholds and third-party access that the frontier safety frameworks and the EU code build on.
