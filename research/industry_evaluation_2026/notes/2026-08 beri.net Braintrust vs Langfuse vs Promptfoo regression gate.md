# Braintrust vs Langfuse vs Promptfoo: Don't Pay for the Gate

## One line
A priced (27 Aug 2026) comparison of eval CI gating showing platform fees range from $0 to ~$1,074/month for the same 600k-score workload, that only Promptfoo fails builds by default, and that none measures judge-vs-human agreement.

## What they do
Workload 400 cases × 3 scorers × 500 runs/month. Pricing and CI behaviours in Braintrust, Langfuse, Opik, Promptfoo notes.

## Evidence
Estimates: Promptfoo $0; Langfuse/Opik self-host $0+infra; Opik Cloud ≈$44; Langfuse Core ≈$115, Pro ≈$285; Braintrust Pro ≈$1,074, Starter ≈$1,476. Judge-model token costs similar across tools and 'often exceed the platform fee'. Cites arXiv 2604.22891 on judge self-preference bias.

## Limitations and criticisms
Independent blog; pricing snapshots change.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Judge token cost dominates; per-score metering penalises adding more judges; calibration vs humans is still a DIY job in most tools.
