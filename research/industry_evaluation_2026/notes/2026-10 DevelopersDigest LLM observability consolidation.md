# Who Bought Your LLM Observability Tool? The 2025-2026 Consolidation

## One line
A dated (18 Oct 2026) tracker of eval/observability M&A — W&B→CoreWeave, Humanloop→Anthropic, Langfuse→ClickHouse, Promptfoo→OpenAI, Helicone→Mintlify, Traceloop→ServiceNow, Galileo→Cisco — with portability advice.

## What they do
Lists each deal with primary links (see individual notes). Also flags non-ownership changes: Datadog 'Agent Observability', PostHog 'AI Observability', Lunary requiring payment for new cloud workspaces, Patronus homepage refocused on RL research. Companion tracker: https://ai-evals.tools/editorial/llm-evals-observability-company-acquisitions (22 May 2026) adds Arize→Velvet and OpenAI→Statsig.

## Evidence
Price estimates for most deals are unverified blog estimates; only W&B and Statsig prices were disclosed.

## Limitations and criticisms
Blog, not primary; Galileo close date conflicts between 'intent' blog and 'completed' claim.

## Relevance to agent evaluation
Advice: instrument once with OTel; keep your own trace copy; keep evals in your repo; prefer MIT/Apache; watch pricing-URL redirects. For agent-judge builders: own the judge and rubric, treat the platform as replaceable.
